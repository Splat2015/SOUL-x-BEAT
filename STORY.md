# SOUL x BEAT — Story Bible

Reference for the RPG / Story Mode expansion. Everything here is decided unless it's marked **TBD**.

---

## 1. World

- The world has **5 distant villages**. Each is home to **polygon-shaped residents** who live in harmony.
- Every village protects and cherishes a large statue called a **Soulcerer** (Soul + Sorcerer).
- Each Soulcerer statue honors a hero who sacrificed themselves in the **great corruption war**, centuries ago.
- **Legend:** after dying in the war, the heroes possessed their statues so they could quietly keep protecting the villages, even as stone.

## 2. The Soulcerers

- All 5 Soulcerers wear **armor**.
- The heroes of Villages 2–5 look almost exactly like the present-day player characters. The player characters are **related** to them.
- The heroes are no longer alive. Their **souls are inside the statues**.

| Village | Soulcerer |
|---|---|
| 1 | **White Star** (identity secret) |
| 2 | Square |
| 3 | Triangle |
| 4 | Pentagon |
| 5 | Circle |

### The White Star

- Village 1 has the fewest residents. Only they recognize the Star hero, and residents of other villages do not.
- The Star hero's true identity stays a **secret for much later**. It may be tied to a findable secret (**TBD**).

## 3. The Tragedy

- A **giant corrupted boss** comes to life and creates havoc.
- Under its influence the villagers slowly turn from pure to **corrupt** again.
- The Soulcerer statues are **held captive** and are slowly being corrupted.
- While corruption is underway, the heroes' souls **cannot leave the statues**. Only stopping the process frees them.
- Only the hero, or several heroes in multiplayer, can save the world from the corruption… or join it.

## 7. Currencies and Rewards

| Currency | Use |
|---|---|
| **Beat Coins** | Like Undertale's gold: buys items, weapons, armor |
| **Soul Relics** | Currency for **one of three shops**, revealed later (**TBD**) |

### Reward rules

- **EXP** is only earned by **fighting**, never by sparing.
- **Village 1:** no enemy or boss gives Soul Relics.
- **From Village 2 onward:** enemies and bosses give Soul Relics, whether you spare them or fight them.
- **Soulcerer bosses** give a generous amount of Beat Coins and Soul Relics, plus EXP if defeated by fighting. Freeing a Soulcerer gives nothing beyond that.

## 8. Routes

| Route | Summary |
|---|---|
| **Pacifist** | Stop the corruption and free the souls without blood on your hands. |
| **Neutral** | Bittersweet, Undertale-style: the world is saved, but with some blood on your hands. |
| **Genocide** | The player abuses their power until they **become the final boss**, almost unstoppable. It is a full alternate route with its own ending. |

### Genocide

- **Trigger, starting in Village 1:** Prism, Pulsar and Maw must **each be defeated 3 times by fighting**, and the Star statue must be defeated once by fighting.
- **The rule continues:** every mini boss (all 39) must be defeated **3 times by fighting**.
- **Rematches** keep the **same stats**, with no scaling. **TBD:** how a rematch is started.
- **Co-op:** **every player** must commit to fighting.
- The corruption comes from the player's own abuse of power, **not** from the final boss.
- **Finale idea:** the **Star hero** fights the player(s) to stop them reaching maximum power and becoming corrupt, as a Sans-equivalent fight.
